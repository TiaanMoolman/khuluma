Setting up eye gaze on the iPad

  1. Mount the iPad securely at the child's eye level, about 30 to 45 cm from the face. Good seating and head support matter more than any setting.
  2. On iPadOS 18 or later: Settings → Accessibility → Eye Tracking. Turn it on and calibrate. Recalibrate when the child's position changes.
  3. For eye gaze, start with the Eye gaze layout and 9 or 16 core words. Move up a grid size as accuracy improves; known words stay in the same place.
  4. Open the board in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen. Turn on Guided Access to keep the child on the board.

The Afrikaans vocabulary and sentence rules should be checked by the child's speech therapist before clinical use. isiZulu and isiXhosa will return in a later version with a researched core vocabulary.
